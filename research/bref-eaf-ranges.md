## 2. How to Use in Damga AI

### 2.1 Material Balance & Yield Range Checks (Red & Yellow Flags)
- **Scrap-to-Liquid-Steel Yield Range:**
  According to Table 8.1 (p. 429), scrap consumption in European EAF plants ranges between **1,039 kg/t LS** and **1,232 kg/t LS** (liquid steel).
  Converting this to metallic yield (1000 / scrap_input):
  - Minimum scrap yield: `1000 / 1232 = 81.17%` (0.812 t LS / t scrap)
  - Maximum scrap yield: `1000 / 1039 = 96.25%` (0.963 t LS / t scrap)
  In modern Turkish and European scrap-EAF operations producing carbon steel long products (billets, rebar, wire rod), typical scrap yield is **89% – 92%** (~1,090 – 1,120 kg scrap per tonne liquid steel). The ~8–11% metallic loss is physically accounted for by:
  1. **Slag oxidation losses (FeO):** 100–150 kg slag/t LS with 10–35 wt-% FeO (~10–40 kg Fe/t LS lost).
  2. **EAF filter dust:** 10–30 kg dust/t LS containing 20–35% Fe and volatile zinc from galvanised scrap (~3–10 kg Fe/t LS lost).
  3. **Combustion / volatilisation of non-metallics:** moisture, oils, coatings, plastics, dirt on scrap.

- **Liquid-Steel-to-Cast-Billet Yield Range:**
  From Section 7.1.5.1 (doc p. 364, referenced by Section 8.1.8 for EAF casting, doc p. 427), continuous casting yields are **"in excess of 95%"**, typically **96.0% – 98.5%**. Physical losses in casting consist of tundish skulls, ladle skulls, cutting crop ends, and strand scale formation (~2.3 – 7.7 kg scale/t LS, Table 7.3).

- **Total Scrap-to-Cast-Billet Yield Range:**
  - Total Yield Range: `[0.8117 * 0.950, 0.9625 * 0.985] = 77.1% to 94.8%` (0.771 - 0.948 t billet / t scrap).
  - Nominal sector benchmark is **87% – 90%** overall yield (e.g. 0.905 * 0.975 = ~88.2%).

- **Validation Rules for Damga AI Engine:**
  - **RED FLAG (Gross Material Balance Discrepancy):**
    Trigger a critical anomaly if reported `Billet Production > Metallic Scrap Input * 0.96` (violates maximum thermodynamic yield) OR if `Billet Production < Metallic Scrap Input * 0.77` (unrealistically high metallic loss > 23%). This flags potential unregistered material diversion, ghost production, or scrap invoice fraud.
  - **YELLOW FLAG (Out-of-Sector Benchmark Warning):**
