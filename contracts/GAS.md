## Economic Assessment for CBAM Reporting

At an average Ethereum Sepolia / Mainnet gas price of 15–20 gwei:
- **Sealing an Attempt**: ~352k gas ≈ $10–$14 (EUR 9–EUR 13).
- **Issuing a Passport (with 2x Groth16 ZK Verifications)**: ~437k gas ≈ $13–$17 (EUR 12–EUR 16).

Compared to the traditional cost of physical CBAM verifications (€5,000 – €15,000 per plant per year), on-chain cryptographic settlement and zero-knowledge verification represent a 99.8% reduction in verification overhead.
